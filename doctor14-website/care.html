Clinical Practice & Services

Areas of Care

Specialized nephrology consultations addressing the full continuum of renal disorders. Dr. Shyma Sunil combines precision diagnostic testing with patient-centered treatment plans to protect and restore your kidney health.

Chronic Kidney Disease involves a gradual loss of kidney function over time, often developing quietly without symptoms until significant filtration capacity has been reduced. Early clinical staging and active intervention can halt or dramatically slow this progression.

Dr. Shyma Sunil conducts comprehensive staging (Stages 1 through 5) based on estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) and Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (uACR). Management emphasizes modern nephroprotective medications (including SGLT2 inhibitors and non-steroidal MRAs), strict blood pressure targets, tailored nutritional counseling, and anemia management.

When kidney function declines to advanced stages (End-Stage Kidney Disease), renal replacement therapy becomes essential to clear metabolic waste, balance electrolytes, and eliminate excess fluid. Dialysis is not an end, but an effective life-sustaining bridge.

Dr. Shyma Sunil guides patients through vascular access planning (early Arteriovenous Fistula creation) for hemodialysis, as well as home-based Continuous Ambulatory Peritoneal Dialysis (CAPD) and Automated Peritoneal Dialysis (APD). Consultations cover dialysis prescription adequacy (Kt/V), dry weight optimization, cardiovascular protection, and bone-mineral disease control.

Kidney transplantation offers the highest quality of life and longest survival outcomes for eligible patients with advanced kidney failure. The journey requires meticulous clinical, immunological, and surgical coordination before and after the operation.

Dr. Shyma Sunil oversees thorough pre-transplant workup for both recipients and living donors (cross-matching, HLA compatibility, cardiovascular clearance) and guides deceased-donor registry enrollment. Lifelong post-transplant care focuses on precise immunosuppressive drug titration, protocol graft biopsies, graft function surveillance, and prevention of opportunistic infections.

Diabetes mellitus and high blood pressure represent the leading dual causes of kidney failure worldwide. Chronically elevated blood sugar and arterial pressure damage the delicate glomeruli, allowing essential proteins to leak into the urine.

Our clinic specializes in early screening for microalbuminuria, glycemic optimization using renally safe therapies, resistant hypertension evaluation (including screening for secondary renovascular causes), and arterial pressure stabilization to shield kidney microvasculature from irreversible scarring.

Nephrolithiasis (kidney stones) causes debilitating flank pain and urinary distress, but more importantly, recurrent stone formation can cause urinary obstruction, chronic infections, and gradual nephron loss.

Beyond acute symptom relief and coordination of urological procedures, Dr. Shyma Sunil performs detailed 24-hour urine metabolic evaluations and stone composition analysis. We formulate tailored preventive regimens—optimizing urinary citrate, correcting hypercalciuria or hyperuricosuria, and designing personalized fluid and mineral intake schedules.

Acute Kidney Injury is an abrupt decline in kidney filtration occurring over hours or days, triggered by severe dehydration, serious infections (sepsis), toxic drug reactions (such as frequent painkiller use), or contrast media.

Unlike chronic disease, AKI is frequently reversible if identified promptly. Dr. Shyma Sunil provides rapid hospital and outpatient intervention, hemodynamic fluid restoration, withdrawal of nephrotoxins, emergency continuous renal replacement therapy (CRRT) when needed, and structured convalescent follow-up to ensure full functional recovery.
